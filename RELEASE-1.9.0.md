# Bite2Eat 1.9.0-alpha.1 — Restaurant Analytics

Adds a database-backed owner analytics dashboard with Today, 7-day, 30-day and 90-day ranges.

Included:
- Revenue, paid order count, average order value and average completion time
- Previous-period percentage comparisons
- Revenue trend chart
- Top-selling menu items with quantity and revenue
- Delivery/collection and card/cash mix
- Peak hour, unique customers and repeat customers
- Live order pipeline totals
- Automatic refresh after live order events
- Owner/manager protected analytics API

Revenue excludes failed, refunded and cancelled orders. Cash and successfully paid card orders are included.
