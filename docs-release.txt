Bite2Eat Web 1.2.0-alpha.1

This release closes the ordering loop with improved checkout validation, a live kitchen workflow and a persistent customer tracking page.

Test flow:
1. Open /r/jimmys and place a demo order.
2. Open the full tracking page from the confirmation screen.
3. Open /r/jimmys/kitchen in a logged-in restaurant session.
4. Accept the order and advance it through Preparing, Ready and Completed.
5. Watch the customer tracking page refresh automatically.
